2 & Problem Analysis: & The problem of detecting cognitive states from EEG signals is analyzed by reviewing existing research and identifying key challenges such as signal noise, artifact removal, and variability across subjects. & 3 \\

3 & Design/development of solutions & A complete EEG-based cognitive assessment system will be designed and developed, covering signal acquisition, feature extraction from frequency bands, and classification using a machine learning model. & 3 \\
\hline
4 & Conduct investigations of complex problems: & EEG data will be collected from subjects performing specific cognitive tasks. The data will be analyzed and used to train and evaluate the classification model. & 2 \\
\hline
5 & Engineering tool usage: & Tools such as Python, MATLAB, and EEG acquisition hardware are used to build and test the system. Libraries like scikit-learn and MNE-Python will be used for signal processing and machine learning. & 2 \\
\hline
6 & The Engineer and the world: & The system has practical applications in healthcare and education, such as monitoring mental workload and detecting early signs of cognitive decline. & 2 \\
\hline
7 & Ethics: & Since the project involves collecting brain signals from human subjects, informed consent is obtained before data collection. Data privacy and responsible use of personal biological data is considered. & 2 \\
\hline
8 & Individual and Team Work: & The project is carried out as a team where different members handled hardware setup, signal processing, and model development. Each member also contributed independently to their assigned tasks. & 2 \\
